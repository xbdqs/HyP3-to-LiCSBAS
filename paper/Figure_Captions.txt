Figure 1. Processing architecture of HyP3-to-LiCSBAS. Source Hybrid Pluggable Processing Pipeline (HyP3) GAMMA products remain read-only. Preflight and planning establish the network, common reference and output grid before raster conversion. The accepted plan is then reused for phase/coherence conversion, baseline and viewing-geometry reconstruction, validation, provenance recording and export to the LiCSBAS2 geocoded interferogram (GEOC) structure.

Figure 2. Semantic mapping between Alaska Satellite Facility (ASF) HyP3 GAMMA inputs and the LiCSBAS2 geocoded interferogram (GEOC) structure. Blue rows denote raster/phase transformations, green rows ancillary or viewing-geometry transformations, orange denotes network reconstruction, and purple denotes metadata/provenance handling. DEM, MLI and E/N/U denote digital elevation model, multilooked intensity and east/north/up components, respectively.

Figure 3. Small-baseline network used for production validation. The stack contains 182 Sentinel-1A ascending acquisitions and 504 interferograms from 26 December 2019 to 30 December 2025. Edge colors distinguish 12-, 24- and 36-day temporal baselines. Perpendicular baselines are expressed relative to 15 January 2023 (star); the open circle marks the degree-one acquisition on 5 September 2022. The network contains one connected component and has cycle rank 323.

Figure 4. Network-wide phase-reference harmonization diagnostics for the production stack. (a) Hexagonal density of the 504 pair-specific HyP3 reference coordinates; the star marks the selected common reference at 126.6676°W, 65.3359°N. (b) Distribution of additive phase shifts applied to place all interferograms on the common datum; the shaded band denotes the central 95% interval and the dashed line the median. (c) Reference-window phase median absolute deviation versus median coherence, colored by temporal-baseline class. All 504 interferograms satisfied the reference-window validity criterion.

Figure 5. Internal consistency of acquisition-level perpendicular-baseline reconstruction. (a) Observed HyP3 pair baselines versus pair baselines reconstructed from the least-squares acquisition solution; the dashed line indicates 1:1 agreement. (b) Reconstruction residuals plotted by interferogram midpoint date. The full-rank solution has an RMS residual of 0.0155 m and a maximum absolute residual of 0.151 m.
